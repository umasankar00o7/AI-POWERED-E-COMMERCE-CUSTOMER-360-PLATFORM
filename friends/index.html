My Story

Passion sewn into every stitch.

We are Ramya and Sailu (RS Sisters). From a young age, we believed in hard work and independence. Rather than being dependents, we chose to carve our own path, turning our passion for fashion. Our YouTube journey began during the Covid period, to teach maggam work to empower others.

This is truly DIY (Do it Your self) channel dedicated to those who are passionate to create something new and creative design
